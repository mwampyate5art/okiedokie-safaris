Tropical Paradise

The Magic of Zanzibar & Swahili Coast

Zanzibar (Unguja) is a legendary archipelago steeped in centuries of spice trade, Arabian architecture, turquoise warm waters, and vibrant marine life.

Bush to Beach: The Ultimate African Contrast

Whether you choose an exclusive private island retreat, an all-inclusive 5-star beachfront resort in Kendwa/Nungwi, or a boutique heritage hotel in the heart of Stone Town, Okie Dokie Safaris curates seamless flight connections, VIP transfers, and bespoke ocean excursions.

  • Nungwi & Kendwa: World-renowned non-tidal white sand beaches swimmable 24 hours a day.
  • Mnemba Atoll Snorkeling: Marine conservation sanctuary with wild dolphins, sea turtles & coral gardens.
  • UNESCO Stone Town: Historic labyrinth of carved wooden doors, the Old Fort, and spice markets.
  • Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park: Sanctuary of the rare endemic Zanzibar Red Colobus monkeys.

5 Days / 4 Nights

5 Days Zanzibar Tropical Escape

The ultimate rejuvenating beach holiday staying in northern Zanzibar (Nungwi or Kendwa), where crystal-clear waters allow swimming at all tides without reef walk-out restrictions.

• Day 1: Airport VIP transfer to beachfront resort, sunset cocktail
• Day 2: Mnemba Atoll dolphin cruise & coral reef snorkeling
• Day 3: Organic Spice Plantation tour & traditional Swahili feast
• Day 4: UNESCO Stone Town guided walking tour & sunset dhow cruise
• Day 5: Leisurely beach morning & transfer to Zanzibar Airport
8 Days / 7 Nights • Best-Seller

8 Days Grand Bush & Beach Safari

The undisputed #1 honeymoon and bucket-list safari in Tanzania. Track the Big Five across Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater, then board a direct bush flight to the white sands of Zanzibar.

• Day 1-2: Tarangire National Park (Elephants, Baobabs & Lions)
• Day 3-4: Serengeti National Park (Great Migration & Big Cats)
• Day 5: Ngorongoro Crater floor game drive; flight to Zanzibar
• Day 6-7: Pure Zanzibar oceanfront relaxation, snorkeling & dhow cruise
• Day 8: Stone Town exploration & transfer to airport
Full Day Ocean Excursion

Safari Blue Dhow & Seafood Island

Zanzibar's most iconic day trip. Set sail on a handcrafted dhow across Menai Bay Marine Conservation Area. Explore sandbanks, snorkel pristine coral reefs, and savor a seafood banquet.

• Morning: Sail to disappearing sandbank with seasonal fresh tropical fruits
• Midday: Guided snorkeling on outer coral reefs with tropical fish
• Lunch: Sumptuous grilled lobster, calamari, jumbo prawns & Swahili rice
• Afternoon: Swim in a natural mangrove lagoon & climb an ancient baobab tree
