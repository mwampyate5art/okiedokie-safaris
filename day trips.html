The Ultimate Kilimanjaro Day Excursion

From the misty rainforest slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro to the sun-drenched savannah oasis of Chemka. A sensory immersion into Chagga tribal traditions, organic coffee craft, and geothermal mineral springs.

From Mountain Waterfall to Desert Oasis

Your journey begins with a seamless VIP pickup from your hotel in Arusha or Moshi. We ascend through picturesque Chagga villages surrounded by lush banana and coffee plantations to reach Materuni Village (2,500m). Enjoy an invigorating guided hike to the base of the 80-meter waterfall, followed by a hands-on coffee workshop where you harvest, roast, and brew your own cup. In the afternoon, cool off at Chemka Hot Springs, an underground geothermal paradise shaded by giant fig trees.

  • Materuni 80m Waterfall: Scenic hike with panoramic mountain views and refreshing pool swim.
  • Chagga Coffee Ceremony: Traditional woodfire roasting, pestle grinding, and tribal songs.
  • Chemka Geothermal Pool: Warm turquoise water (28°C), rope swings, and natural doctor fish.
  • Hot Chagga Lunch: Authentic Machalari, grilled chicken, fresh avocado & tropical fruits.

Detailed Schedule

The Complete Full-Day Dual Experience

Convenient morning pickup from your hotel in Arusha or Moshi at 8:30 AM in a private air-conditioned vehicle with your dedicated senior guide.

Morning: 9:00 AM – 1:30 PM

1. Materuni Waterfalls & Chagga Coffee

Located in the authentic village of Materuni, 2,500m up the slopes of Kilimanjaro. Enjoy a gentle 45-minute guided hike passing coffee and banana groves to the foot of the 80-meter waterfall. Take photos, feel the cooling mountain mist, and dip your feet in the mountain plunge pool.

Organic Coffee Workshop:

Learn how coffee beans are harvested, pulped, and dried. Roast your beans over an open woodfire, grind them in giant wooden mortars while chanting traditional Chagga harvest songs, and sip the freshest, most aromatic cup of coffee of your life!

Traditional Lunch: Hot cooked Chagga buffet (Machalari, stewed chicken, savory plantains & seasonal avocado).
Afternoon: 2:00 PM – 5:30 PM

2. Chemka (Kikuletwa) Hot Springs Oasis

Drive across the semi-arid savannah into a hidden paradise surrounded by gigantic twisting fig trees and dangling roots. Chemka's geothermal spring pumps crystal-clear, pleasantly warm turquoise mineral water (28°C) from deep underground volcanic aquifers.

Highlights at the Oasis:

  • Swimming in warm, crystal-clear turquoise mineral water
  • Swinging from the famous tree rope swing into deep waters
  • Natural doctor fish spa (tiny fish gently nibbling your toes)
  • Relaxing in shaded hammocks beneath colossal fig canopies
  • Chilled beverages, fresh coconuts & local barbecue bites
5:30 PM Transfer: Relaxing private air-conditioned transfer back to your hotel in Arusha or Moshi.
