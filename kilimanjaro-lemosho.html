Route Overview:

The Lemosho Route is widely considered one of the most scenic paths up Mount Kilimanjaro. Starting on the pristine western slopes, it passes through untouched rainforests, crosses the expansive Shira Plateau, and offers an exceptional acclimatization profile for maximum summit success.

Complete Itinerary

Day-by-Day Expedition Schedule

DAY 1

Arusha to Londorossi Gate to Mti Mkubwa Camp

In the morning, your Okie Dokie Safaris guide will pick you up from Seven Skies Hotel in Arusha/Moshi for a scenic drive to Londorossi Gate for registration. We then drive further to the Lemosho trailhead. The trek begins through pristine, lush rainforest where sightings of Colobus monkeys and exotic birdlife are frequent. We reach Mti Mkubwa (Big Tree) Camp by late afternoon.

Elevation: 2,100m / 6,889ft to 2,650m / 8,694ft
Distance: 6 km / 4 miles
Hiking Time: 3–4 hours
Habitat: Rainforest
DAY 2

Mti Mkubwa Camp to Shira 2 Camp

We ascend out of the rainforest zone into the giant heather and moorland. The trail steepens as we cross the plateau ridge, offering panoramic views of the Shira Plateau—one of the highest plateaus on Earth. We continue across the plateau to Shira 2 Camp for a well-deserved rest and warm dinner.

Elevation: 2,650m / 8,694ft to 3,840m / 12,598ft
Distance: 16 km / 10 miles
Hiking Time: 7–8 hours
Habitat: Moorland / Heath
DAY 3 - ACCLIMATIZATION

Shira 2 Camp to Lava Tower to Barranco Camp (Acclimatization Day)

A crucial day for acclimatization! We trek east up a stony ridge toward the massive Lava Tower (4,630m) for lunch. Following the "climb high, sleep low" rule, we then descend into the beautiful Barranco Valley. This helps your body adjust to high altitude and dramatically improves summit success.

Elevation: 3,840m to Lava Tower (4,630m / 15,190ft) to Barranco (3,960m)
Distance: 10 km / 6 miles
Hiking Time: 6–8 hours
Habitat: Alpine Desert
DAY 4

Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp (Barranco Wall)

The morning begins with an exhilarating climb up the famous Barranco Wall. While it looks steep, it is a non-technical scramble that rewards trekkers with breathtaking views from the top. We then traverse a series of hills and gullies into Karanga Valley Camp for lunch and an afternoon of relaxation.

Elevation: 3,960m / 12,992ft to 3,995m / 13,106ft
Distance: 5 km / 3 miles
Hiking Time: 4–5 hours
Habitat: Alpine Desert
DAY 5

Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp (Base Camp)

We leave Karanga and ascend toward Barafu Camp, completing the South Circuit of Kilimanjaro. Upon reaching base camp, our team sets up tents while you enjoy an early dinner and prepare your gear for the midnight summit push. Rest and sleep as much as possible before midnight.

Elevation: 3,995m / 13,106ft to 4,673m / 15,331ft
Distance: 4 km / 2.5 miles
Hiking Time: 3–4 hours
Habitat: Alpine Desert
DAY 6 - SUMMIT DAY!

Barafu Camp to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp

At midnight, we begin our climb to the summit under the stars! Ascending steep scree, we reach Stella Point (5,756m) on the crater rim in time for a glorious sunrise. A final 1-hour trek along the snow-capped rim brings you to Uhuru Peak (5,895m) — The Roof of Africa! After photos and celebration, we descend back to Barafu for a quick rest and warm meal, then continue down to Mweka Camp for the night.

Elevation: 4,673m to Uhuru Peak (5,895m) down to Mweka (3,100m)
Distance: 5 km ascent / 12 km descent (17 km total)
Hiking Time: 6–8 hrs ascent / 5–7 hrs descent
Habitat: Arctic Summit / Rain Forest Boundary
DAY 7

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate to Arusha

After breakfast and a joyous celebration song with your mountain crew, we descend through the rainforest to Mweka Gate. Here, you will be awarded your official Kilimanjaro Summit Certificates! Our driver will be waiting to transport you back to Seven Skies Hotel in Arusha for a hot shower and celebration.

Elevation: 3,100m / 10,170ft to 1,640m / 5,380ft
Distance: 10 km / 6 miles
Hiking Time: 3–4 hours
Habitat: Rainforest

Departure or Onward Safari:

After breakfast at Seven Skies Hotel, check out and transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your flight back home, or continue your adventure with an Okie Dokie Safaris wildlife safari into Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater!
