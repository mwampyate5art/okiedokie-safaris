Route Overview:

The 7-Day Machame Route (popularly known as the "Whiskey Route") offers spectacular views, high summit success rates due to its excellent "climb high, sleep low" acclimatization profile, and diverse landscapes ranging from dense rainforests to alpine deserts.

Arrival : Welcome to Tanzania (Arrival & Pre-Trek Briefing)

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) or Arusha Airport, you will be met by our Okie Dokie Safaris representative and transferred to Seven Skies Hotel in Arusha. In the afternoon, you will meet your Lead Mountain Guide for a full trip briefing and equipment check to ensure you are fully prepared for the trek. Accommodation: Seven Skies Hotel (Bed & Breakfast / Half Board) Meals: Dinner Included.

Complete Itinerary

Day-by-Day Expedition Schedule

DAY 1

Arusha to Machame Gate to Machame Camp

After breakfast, we drive from Arusha through vibrant local villages to the Machame Gate. After park registration and meeting our mountain crew, we begin our trek through the lush, dense rainforest. Keep an eye out for Black and White Colobus monkeys along the trail!

Elevation: 1,800m / 5,905ft to 3,010m / 9,875ft
Distance: 11 km / 7 miles
Hiking Time: 5–7 hours
Habitat: Rainforest
DAY 2

Machame Camp to Shira 2 Camp

We leave the rainforest behind and ascend steep paths through the moorland zone. The trek offers breathtaking open views of the Shira Plateau and the majestic Kibo Peak ahead. We reach Shira 2 Camp in time for lunch and a restful afternoon.

Elevation: 3,010m / 9,875ft to 3,840m / 12,598ft
Distance: 5 km / 3 miles
Hiking Time: 4–6 hours
Habitat: Moorland
DAY 3 - ACCLIMATIZATION

Shira 2 Camp to Lava Tower to Barranco Camp (Acclimatization Day)

A key day for acclimatization! We trek east up a stony ridge toward the towering Lava Tower (4,630m) for lunch. Afterwards, we descend into the scenic Barranco Valley. This "climb high, sleep low" strategy greatly aids your body in adapting to altitude.

Elevation: 3,840m to Lava Tower (4,630m / 15,190ft) to Barranco (3,960m)
Distance: 10 km / 6 miles
Hiking Time: 6–8 hours
Habitat: Alpine Desert
DAY 4

Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp (Barranco Wall)

We begin the morning by tackling the iconic Barranco Wall—an exciting scramble that rewards you with unbelievable views at the top. From there, we cross a series of hills and valleys into Karanga Valley Camp, setting up for a short day that conserves energy for the summit push.

Elevation: 3,960m / 12,992ft to 3,995m / 13,106ft
Distance: 5 km / 3 miles
Hiking Time: 4–5 hours
Habitat: Alpine Desert
DAY 5

Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp (Base Camp)

We trek up to Barafu Camp, completing the South Circuit of Mount Kilimanjaro. Upon arrival, we set up camp, enjoy an early dinner, and rest as much as possible before midnight. Tonight is the summit push!

Elevation: 3,995m / 13,106ft to 4,673m / 15,331ft
Distance: 4 km / 2.5 miles
Hiking Time: 3–4 hours
Habitat: Alpine Desert
DAY 6 - SUMMIT DAY!

Barafu Camp to Uhuru Peak to Mweka Camp

At midnight, we begin our push to the roof of Africa! Walking by headlamp, we ascend steep scree toward Stella Point (5,756m) on the crater rim to witness an unforgettable sunrise. A final 1-hour push brings you to Uhuru Peak (5,895m) — The Highest Point in Africa! After celebrating and taking photos, we descend to Barafu for a quick lunch and short rest, then continue down to Mweka Camp for a well-deserved sleep.

Elevation: 4,673m to Uhuru Peak (5,895m / 19,341ft) down to Mweka (3,100m)
Distance: 5 km ascent / 12 km descent (17 km total)
Hiking Time: 6–8 hrs ascent / 5–7 hrs descent
Habitat: Arctic Summit / Rain Forest Boundary
DAY 7

Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate to Arusha

After breakfast and a joyous tipping/singing ceremony with your mountain crew, we descend through the rainforest to Mweka Gate. Here, you will collect your official Kilimanjaro Summit Certificates! Our vehicle will be waiting to transfer you back to Seven Skies Hotel in Arusha for a long-awaited hot shower and relaxation.

Elevation: 3,100m / 10,170ft to 1,640m / 5,380ft
Distance: 10 km / 6 miles
Hiking Time: 3–4 hours
Habitat: Rainforest

Departure or Onward Safari:

After breakfast at Seven Skies Hotel, check out and transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your flight back home, or continue your adventure with an Okie Dokie Safaris wildlife safari into Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater!
