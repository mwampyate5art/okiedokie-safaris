Route Overview:

The Rongai Route is the only trail that approaches Mount Kilimanjaro from the northern side, near the Kenyan border. It offers a remote, less crowded wilderness experience, passes through pine forests, and has a drier micro-climate, making it the best option during wet seasons.

Complete Itinerary

Day-by-Day Expedition Schedule

DAY 1

(Arusha / Moshi) Hotel to Rongai Gate to Simba Camp

In the morning, your Okie Dokie Safaris guide will pick you up from Seven Skies Hotel in Arusha for a drive around the eastern base of the mountain to Rongai Gate. After registration, the trek begins through pine forests and farmland before opening into montane forest. We arrive at Simba Camp near the edge of the moorland zone.

Elevation: 1,950m / 6,398ft to 2,600m / 8,530ft
Distance: 7 km / 4 miles
Hiking Time: 3–4 hours
Habitat: Montane Forest / Moorland
DAY 2

Simba Camp to Kikelelwa Camp

We steadily ascend through the moorland, enjoying dramatic views of Kibo Peak and the Kenyan plains below. After a brief lunch stop at Second Cave Camp (3,450m), we branch off toward the jagged spires of Mawenzi Peak, reaching Kikelelwa Camp nestled in a sheltered valley.

Elevation: 2,600m / 8,530ft to 3,600m / 11,811ft
Distance: 12 km / 7.5 miles
Hiking Time: 6–7 hours
Habitat: Moorland
DAY 3

Kikelelwa Camp to Mawenzi Tarn Camp

A short but steep climb takes us up grassy slopes rewarded by sweeping views. We setup camp at Mawenzi Tarn, situated beneath the towering sheer cliffs of Mawenzi Peak. The afternoon is spent resting or taking an acclimatization walk around the tarn lake.

Elevation: 3,600m / 11,811ft to 4,330m / 14,206ft
Distance: 5 km / 3 miles
Hiking Time: 3–4 hours
Habitat: Alpine Desert
DAY 4

Mawenzi Tarn Camp to Kibo Hut

We traverse the high alpine desert known as "The Saddle," which spans the vast plateau between Mawenzi and Kibo. We arrive at Kibo Hut early in the afternoon, eat an early dinner, and go to bed early to prepare for the midnight summit bid.

Elevation: 4,330m / 14,206ft to 4,700m / 15,419ft
Distance: 8 km / 5 miles
Hiking Time: 5–6 hours
Habitat: Alpine Desert
DAY 5 - SUMMIT DAY!

Kibo Hut to Uhuru Peak to Horombo Hut

At midnight, we begin our steep ascent over scree paths to Gillman’s Point (5,685m) on the crater rim. Continuing along the snow-covered rim, we reach Uhuru Peak (5,895m) — The Roof of Africa! After celebrating at the summit, we descend to Kibo Hut for lunch and then down to Horombo Hut for a restful night.

Elevation: 4,700m to Uhuru Peak (5,895m / 19,341ft) down to Horombo (3,720m)
Distance: 6 km ascent / 16 km descent (22 km total)
Hiking Time: 6–8 hrs ascent / 6–7 hrs descent
Habitat: Arctic Summit / Alpine Desert
Accommodation: Horombo Wooden Huts
DAY 6

Horombo Hut to Marangu Gate to Arusha

Our final trek descends through moorland and dense rainforest to Marangu Gate. You will collect your official Kilimanjaro Summit Certificates before meeting your transfer vehicle back to Seven Skies Hotel in Arusha / Moshi.

Elevation: 3,720m / 12,205ft to 1,870m / 6,135ft
Distance: 20 km / 12.5 miles
Hiking Time: 5–7 hours
Habitat: Rainforest

Departure or Onward Safari:

After breakfast at Seven Skies Hotel, check out and transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your flight back home, or continue your adventure with an Okie Dokie Safaris wildlife safari into Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater!
