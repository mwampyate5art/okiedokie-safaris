Discover Kilimanjaro

Everything About Mount Kilimanjaro

Mount Kilimanjaro rises majestically above the East African plains to 5,895 meters (19,341 feet). Composed of three distinct volcanic cones — Kibo, Mawenzi, and Shira — it is recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

A Journey Through Earth's Biomes

Every expedition begins in the warm agricultural foothills and tropical rainforest, home to blue monkeys and black-and-white Colobus monkeys. As you ascend, you cross the surreal moorland dotted with giant Groundsels and Lobelias, traverse the moon-like alpine desert of The Saddle, and finally stand upon the arctic glaciers of Uhuru Peak as the African sun rises above the sea of clouds.

  • Peak Elevation: 5,895m (19,341 ft) at Uhuru Peak.
  • Location: Kilimanjaro National Park, Northern Tanzania.
  • Climbing Style: Non-technical trekking suitable for determined adventurers.
  • Safety Standard: Daily pulse oximeter monitoring & emergency oxygen on every climb.

Sleeping Huts

1. Marangu Route

6 Days / 5 Nights • The "Coca-Cola" Route

The oldest and classic trail on Kilimanjaro. It is the only route offering comfortable sleeping huts with solar-powered lights and beds, making it ideal for trekkers who prefer not to camp in tents.

Duration: 6 Days
Lodging: Mandara & Horombo Huts
Difficulty: Moderate
VIP Rate: From $2,600 / Pax
Explore Marangu Route
Northern Approach

2. Rongai Route

6 Days / 5 Nights • Remote Wilderness

The only trail that approaches Mount Kilimanjaro from the northern side near the Kenyan border. Uncrowded wilderness, pine forests, drier micro-climate, and stunning views beneath Mawenzi Peak.

Duration: 6 Days
Lodging: Mountain Camps
Difficulty: Moderate
VIP Rate: From $2,850 / Pax
Explore Rongai Route
High Success Rate

3. Machame Route

7 Days / 6 Nights • The "Whiskey" Route

The iconic trail renowned for scenic variety, excellent "climb high, sleep low" acclimatization via Lava Tower, and the thrilling Barranco Wall scramble. Offers top summit success rates.

Duration: 7 Days VIP
Lodging: Mountain Camps
Difficulty: Strenuous
VIP Rate: From $2,950 / Pax
Explore Machame Route
Most Scenic Route

4. Lemosho Route

7 Days / 6 Nights • Untouched Western Slopes

Widely regarded as the most picturesque route on Kilimanjaro. Starts in pristine western rainforests, crosses the vast Shira Plateau, and provides gradual altitude adaptation with stunning vistas.

Duration: 7 Days VIP
Lodging: Mountain Camps
Difficulty: Moderate-Strenuous
VIP Rate: From $3,050 / Pax
