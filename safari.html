7-Day Detailed Day-by-Day Expedition Itinerary

DAY 1 Arusha to Tarangire National Park – Karatu Highlands
Baobabs & Massive Elephant Herds
Kingdom of Elephants, Colossal Baobabs & Rift Escarpment

Your Okie Dokie Safaris adventure begins with morning pickup from your hotel in Arusha or Kilimanjaro International Airport. Following an expedition briefing, drive directly to Tarangire National Park, celebrated worldwide for its ancient thousand-year-old colossal baobab trees and Africa's densest concentration of elephants. Embark on a full-day thrilling game drive along the Tarangire River basin where hundreds of elephants dig for underground water alongside lions, leopards, buffalos, giraffes, and over 550 bird species. Enjoy an exquisite bush picnic lunch inside the park. In late afternoon, journey up through the scenic Great Rift Valley escarpment into the cooler green highlands of Karatu for a relaxing dinner and overnight, significantly shortening tomorrow’s drive into the Serengeti.

Accommodation: Tarangire Sopa Lodge / Maramboi Tented Camp (or Marera Valley Lodge / Farm of Dreams)
Meals: Lunch (Picnic Bush Box), Gourmet Dinner
Drive Time: ~2.5 hrs to Tarangire, ~2 hrs to Karatu
DAY 2 Karatu – Central Serengeti (Seronera Valley Heartland)
Africa's Big Cat Predator Capital
Ngorongoro Highlands Transit & The Endless Golden Savannah

Following an early hearty breakfast, depart Karatu and ascend through the breathtaking misty cloud forests of the Ngorongoro Conservation Area rim, stopping at the crater viewpoint for jaw-dropping panoramic vistas. Descend onto the vast golden savannah of Central Serengeti (Seronera Valley) — derived from the Maasai word "Siringet" meaning "the land that runs on forever." The Seronera River network provides year-round water, making this region Africa’s premier big cat territory. Enjoy an action-packed en-route game drive tracking prides of black-maned lions lounging on granite kopjes, elusive leopards draped gracefully across acacia boughs, and cheetahs sprinting after gazelles. Arrive at your luxury tented lodge in the late afternoon for sundowners by the campfire.

Accommodation: Into Wild Serengeti Tented Camp / Kubu Kubu Tented Lodge (or similar luxury camp)
Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Gourmet Bush Picnic Lunch, Multi-Course Dinner)
Wildlife Focus: Lions on Kopjes, Tree Leopards, Cheetahs, Hyenas, Topis
DAY 3 Central Serengeti to North Serengeti (Kogatende & Mara River)
Dramatic Great Migration River Crossings
Heart of the Great Migration & Thrilling Mara River Crossings

After breakfast, embark on a scenic game drive heading north through the Lobo wildlife corridor toward North Serengeti (Kogatende). You are now positioned at the absolute epicenter of the world-famous Great Wildebeest Migration! Spend the day tracking immense herds of over 1.5 million wildebeest and 300,000 zebras gathering on the banks of the mighty Mara River. Watch tension mount as thousands of nervous animals hesitate on steep riverbanks before plunging into surging waters fraught with colossal Nile crocodiles. Witness nature's raw survival instinct unfold before your cameras. Return to your premium wilderness tented camp as the sun sets over the Mara River basin for dinner and stories around the bushfire.

Accommodation: Premium Wilderness Serengeti Camp / Mara Mara Tented Lodge (or Koga Tented Camp)
Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Picnic Lunch on Riverbank, Dinner Under Stars)
Highlights: Mara River Crossings, Giant Nile Crocodiles, North Serengeti Plains
DAY 4 North Serengeti (Sunrise Balloon Safari) – Ngorongoro Crater Rim
$570 Hot Air Balloon & Champagne Breakfast Included
Floating Above the Wild Plains at Sunrise & Crater Rim Sunset

An unforgettable highlight! Wake up before dawn (approx. 5:00 AM) for transfer to the launch site. Board your Serengeti Hot Air Balloon (a premium $570 experience fully included in your package tariff). Gently rise into the pink African dawn, silently floating above the boundless wilderness as herds of wildebeest, giraffes, and zebras awaken below. Upon touchdown, celebrate with a ceremonial bush champagne uncorking followed by a 5-star English breakfast cooked fresh beneath an acacia tree. Afterward, enjoy a final morning predator tracking drive before journeying southward across the Serengeti plains toward the high-altitude rim of Ngorongoro Crater. Check into your crater rim lodge with jaw-dropping sunset vistas over the caldera.

Accommodation: Rhino Lodge / Ngorongoro Serena Safari Lodge (or Marera Valley Lodge)
Meals: Full Board (Champagne Bush Breakfast, Picnic Lunch, Luxury Crater Dinner)
Special Inclusion: Serengeti Balloon Safari ($570 value) + Flight Certificate
DAY 5 Ngorongoro Crater Tour – Karatu / Lake Eyasi
600m Floor Descent & Endangered Black Rhinos
The 8th Wonder of the World & The Complete African Big Five

Descend early in the morning through winding rainforest tracks 600 meters down onto the caldera floor of the world-famous Ngorongoro Crater. This ancient collapsed volcano covers 260 km² and supports an astonishing concentration of over 25,000 large mammals. This is your premier destination to encounter the highly elusive and critically endangered Eastern Black Rhino, alongside massive old tusker elephants, dense lion prides, cheetahs, jackals, hyenas, and wallowing hippos. Enjoy an idyllic picnic lunch on the shores of the Ngoitoktok Hippo Pool as hippos grumble in the water. In late afternoon, ascend the steep crater walls and drive down into the Lake Eyasi basin to check into your cultural tented lodge.

Accommodation: Lake Eyasi Premium Tented Lodge / Kisima Ngeda Camp (or Lake Karatu Safari Lodge)
Meals: Full Board (Breakfast, Crater Floor Picnic Lunch, Lodge Dinner)
Wildlife Focus: Black Rhino, Big Five, Lake Magadi Flamingos, Ngoitoktok Hippos
DAY 6 Lake Eyasi Cultural Experience – Hadzabe Bushmen & Datoga Tribe
Authentic Bushmen Hunting & Ancient Metallurgy
Ancient Hunter-Gatherer Bow Hunting & Datoga Blacksmith Craft

Before the sun rises, venture into the rugged acacia bush of Lake Eyasi to join the Hadzabe tribe — one of the very last authentic hunter-gatherer societies remaining on the planet. For over 10,000 years, the Hadzabe have lived entirely off the land with zero modern agriculture. Communicate using their ancient click language, learn how to ignite fires using friction sticks in seconds, and accompany the hunters through the thornbush tracking wild game using handmade poison-tipped bows and arrows. Witness their incredible botanical wisdom and wild honey collection. Later, visit the neighboring Datoga pastoralists and skilled blacksmiths. Watch them smelt scrap metal over charcoal embers to forge hunting arrowheads, spear tips, and brass bracelets. A profound cultural journey through human history.

Accommodation: Lake Eyasi Tented Camp / Farm of Dreams Lodge (or Lake Karatu Safari Lodge)
Meals: Full Board (Bush Breakfast, Traditional Lunch, Lodge Dinner)
Cultural Immersion: Hadzabe Bow Hunt, Click Language, Datoga Blacksmith Metalcraft
DAY 7 Lake Natron Pink Flamingos & Ol Doinyo Lengai – Departure
2.5 Million Lesser Flamingos & Mountain of God
Great Rift Valley Mineral Flats & Return to Arusha / Airport

Depart early morning traversing the dramatic Great Rift Valley basin toward Lake Natron, an ethereal, highly alkaline soda lake glowing in deep crimson and pink hues. Lake Natron is the only regular breeding ground in East Africa for 2.5 million lesser flamingos! Stroll along the surreal mineral crust flats with your guide, taking breathtaking photographs of flamingo flocks against the looming conical silhouette of Ol Doinyo Lengai — the active "Mountain of God" revered by the Maasai people. Enjoy a scenic hot lunch before relaxing during the return drive back to Arusha or direct transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) for your evening departure flight, concluding your journey of a lifetime.

Departure: Transfer to Arusha Hotel / Kilimanjaro Airport (JRO)
Meals: Breakfast, Scenic Lunch
Highlights: 2.5M Flamingos, Ol Doinyo Lengai Volcano, Rift Valley Scenery

20,226 km² Lion Heartland

Ruaha National Park

10% of World's Wild Lions & Raw Wilderness

Tanzania's second-largest national park and a remote wilderness dream. Ruaha harbors 10% of the entire planet's remaining wild lions, celebrated for pride super-groups numbering up to 30 individuals. Its rugged baobab landscape alongside the Great Ruaha River is home to both Greater and Lesser Kudu.

Western Safari Circuit

Gombe • Mahale Mountains • Katavi • Lake Tanganyika
52 km² Jane Goodall Legacy

Gombe Stream National Park

World's Premier Chimpanzee Trekking Haven

World-renowned as the living laboratory where Dr. Jane Goodall pioneered modern primatology beginning in 1960. Located on the crystal-clear shores of Lake Tanganyika, Gombe offers intimate on-foot encounters with habituated wild chimpanzees, olive baboons, red-tailed monkeys, and scenic waterfalls.

412 km² Garden of God

Kitulo National Park (Bustani ya Mungu)

The Serengeti of Flowers & Wild Orchids

Known locally in Kiswahili as *"Bustani ya Mungu"* (The Garden of God). Located in the volcanic Southern Highlands at 2,600m, it is the first national park in tropical Africa protected specifically for its rare alpine flora. Boasts over 350 species of wildflowers including 45 terrestrial orchid varieties.

🌺 45+ Wild Orchid Species 🏔️ 2,600m High Plateau 🦋 Rare Alpine Butterflies 📸 Botanical Photo Tours
Itinerary in Package: 3-4 Day Botanical Wildflower Tour Expedition Role: Walking trek across the 2,600m high-altitude plateau, discovering 45+ wild orchid species and over 350 wildflowers in "The Garden of God." Lodges: Mbeya Hotel / Matamba Eco-Campsite
